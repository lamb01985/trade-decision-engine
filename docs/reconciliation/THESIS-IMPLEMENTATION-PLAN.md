# EdgeIQ / Thesis implementation plan

Working brand: EdgeIQ. Tagline: Find your edge. Browser title: EdgeIQ | Investment Intelligence. Thesis is the research engine, Radar discovery, Compare comparisons, Valuation Lab modeling, Trading Desk existing tools. Brand strings stay centralized/changeable; do not rename repositories, internal routes, environment variables, storage keys or deployment projects. Apply branding only after preservation/reconciliation passes.

## Preserved foundation and roadmap

Keep every Trading Desk feature and original storage semantics. Security key retirement, exact provider allowlists, per-product access states and common account admission remain mandatory. Long Opportunities and Short Opportunities are peers. Later increments add segment/earnings inflections, valuation-versus-growth and revision/catalyst discovery; Distress Radar adds cash runway, maturity/refinancing/covenant/going-concern evidence; Valuation Lab adds revenue/EPS/DCF/reverse valuation/scenario/sensitivity models. Claude Deep Research interprets source-backed evidence with bull/bear rebuttals and cost control, never invents metrics. Monitoring adds durable watchlists, thesis versions, alerts and outcome history. Execution Readiness remains separate and requires verified price/chain/borrow/depth/size evidence. Preserve Issue4 liquidity defaults and point-in-time successful/failed bearish validation requirements. No deployment is authorized.

## First bounded slice

Implement Company Intelligence plus deterministic long/short financial scores and a basic comparison of at most three companies. US-GAAP nonfinancial annual10-K/10-K-A statements only. Show four fiscal-year records when available, source filing dates, fiscal periods, formulas, assumptions, limitations, confidence and counterarguments. Valuation and entry attractiveness explicitly UNKNOWN without verified price, forecasts and execution evidence. Do not substitute a financial score for business moat, investment return, bankruptcy probability or trade readiness.

Backend: new app/thesis/{financials,sec,api,fixtures,dev_app}.py in the existing Python repository; include protected router in app/main.py. Add no dependencies, database migration or changes to frozen technical rules. SEC source uses server-only fixed hosts/company_tickers + submissions + companyfacts, configured SEC_USER_AGENT and THESIS_SEC_FETCH_ENABLED=true. No arbitrary URL input or provider calls in tests. Single-process1request/second scheduler, coalesced bounded cache, no retries, ten-second absolute HTTP/body deadlines, byte limits and25second company deadline; hosting stays one worker until shared scheduling is reviewed. Unknown symbols/filings return typed unavailable, never a different company or synthetic price.

GET /api/v1/thesis/company?symbol=...&as_of=YYYY-MM-DD requires configured MARKET_DATA_SERVICE_TOKEN with exact X-TradeHub-Audience:thesis-v1, constant-time bearer comparison and explicit THESIS_ENABLED=true before cache/provider access. Dedicated server service URL/token/profile names are reused; SEC-only financial analysis does not require Massive quote entitlements or silently enable its service. Validate versioned response identity and shape before browser display.

Normalization selects filings known by the requested as_of date, never future filings. Group by actual fiscal start/end, not reported fiscal-year labels on comparative facts. Annual duration330–380days, supported form10-K/10-K-A, USD units only; instant balance facts must match the annual end. Prefer latest eligible filed/accession evidence for restatements. Ambiguous same-rank conflicting facts remain unavailable. Require consecutive annual ends330–400days for growth. Revenue tags are explicit aliases; CFO and capital-expenditure purchase tags are separate. FCF=CFO minus capital purchases, never all investing cash flow. Null/missing/zero/negative values are distinct; booleans and nonfinite values are invalid. Ratios use matching periods/units. Negative/zero denominator ratios are unavailable, not infinity. SIC6000–6799 (including financials/REITs) disables generic traffic-light scoring; unknown sector gets an explicit applicability warning.

Metrics: annual revenue, net income, CFO, capex, FCF, cash, debt (only when both current/noncurrent components observed), assets, liabilities, equity, current assets/liabilities, diluted EPS; net margin, revenue YoY, prior YoY, acceleration, margin change, cash conversion, current ratio and debt/assets. Each derived metric names formula and filing inputs. No TTM or quarterly run-rate claim. Annual freshness is explicit; >550days from latest annual period is stale and blocks scores; recent quarterly information is not assessed. Confidence is LOW with missing/stale/unsupported inputs, otherwise at most MEDIUM because this is an annual quantitative screen.

Assessments: financial-quality cash conversion>=1green,>=0.7yellow,else red (positive earnings required; losses red); profitability margin>=10percentgreen,>0yellow,else red; growth positive YoY>=10percent and nonnegative acceleration green,otherpositiveyellow,else red; financial health current ratio>=1.5 and debt/assets<=0.5 green,current ratio<1 or equity<=0 red,else yellow; required missing facts yield UNKNOWN. Separate valuation/entry remain UNKNOWN. Each assessment includes formula, reasons, assumptions and a plausible alternative explanation. Long score is fixed25points per dimension (green25/yellow12.5/red0), requires all four; do not renormalize missing coverage. Short deterioration score uses revenue decline30, acceleration below-5percentagepoints20, margin contraction below-2points20, negativeFCF15, red financial health15; require all five inputs, else null with coverage. It is not inverse long quality, overvaluation or short profitability. Every score shows policy version and excludes unsupported/stale cases.

Company input/as_of selection, report cards with six dimension states, metric evidence, source links and a side-by-side Compare table. Async generations clear old selection/results; failures cannot resurrect prior companies. New workspace available only via authenticated server feature status; default off. ## Normative implementation-review resolutions

Concept table (us-gaap; alias order only breaks ties after newest eligible filed/accession ranking): revenue RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax/Revenues/SalesRevenueNet; net_income NetIncomeLoss/ProfitLoss; cfo NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities; capex PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment (nonnegative purchase amount; negative observations invalid); cash CashAndCashEquivalentsAtCarryingValue; assets Assets; liabilities Liabilities; equity StockholdersEquity/StockholdersEquityIncludingPortionAttributableToNoncontrollingInterest; current_assets AssetsCurrent; current_liabilities LiabilitiesCurrent; debt_current LongTermDebtCurrent/LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligationsCurrent; debt_noncurrent LongTermDebtNoncurrent; eps EarningsPerShareDiluted (basic EPS is not a diluted fallback). All use USD except EPS USD/shares. Debt is sum of both observed current/noncurrent components; it excludes unreported other financing and is labeled limited debt proxy. Conflicting aliases/duplicate facts at identical latest rank become unavailable rather than using precedence to hide disagreement. Balance evidence must match selected revenue period end; duration evidence must match exact start/end. Values must be finite real numbers, not booleans. Every selected fact has a valid filed date<=as_of and accession matching ten digits-two digits-six digits. Filter unsupported forms before ranking. Annual period dates, not comparative fy labels, own identity.

Missing-input precedence is first. Financial health then tests equity<=0/currentratio<1 RED before GREEN conditions; zero/negative current liabilities make ratio unknown. Quality is RED for net_income<=0 if income/CFO known, otherwise cash conversion positive-income rule. All ratios require positive denominators. Growth GREEN requires observed acceleration as well as YoY; missing required acceleration UNKNOWN. Score/staleness age uses requested as_of minus latest annual period end, not wall-clock now; as_of defaults UTC today, rejects future dates, no intraday point-in-time claim. At most four annual periods; adjacent ends330–400days required. Unsupported financial SIC or stale periods block all traffic lights/scores but retain dated raw facts. Unknown SIC makes applicability/confidence warning explicit.
